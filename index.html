"We wish to see them happy always through unstructured learning... and yes, they are happy indeed!"

Welcome to First Steps Pre School! It brings me immense joy to see young minds blossom every day in our nurturing environment. We strongly believe that before children master letters and numbers, they must fall deeply in love with learning through touch, laughter, imagination, and play.

This year, we are thrilled to expand our nature discovery and sensory exploration activities to connect little ones with their environment while instilling kindness, confidence, and curiosity.
