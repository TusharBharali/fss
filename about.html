About First Steps Pre School in Tezpur

"We wish to see them happy always through unstructured learning... and yes, they are happy indeed!"

Our Founder's Heart

A Loving Haven in Tezpur

First Steps was founded with a singular, joyful dream: to create a preschool where childhood is not rushed, but cherished. Located on Mazgaon Main Road in Tezpur, Assam, our center has blossomed into one of the most trusted early learning institutions in the region.

We know that the earliest years form the foundation of a child's confidence, emotional resilience, and love for knowledge. That is why our curriculum moves beyond rigid instruction and embraces unstructured, child-centric exploration.

Here, children touch, smell, build, sing, and ask questions. They connect with nature, celebrate cultural roots, and discover that they are capable of amazing things!

Our Methodology

The Magic of Unstructured Play

Why play-based discovery prepares young minds better than traditional memorization.

🎨

Imagination Without Limits

Instead of coloring inside prescribed lines, our little ones mix paints, sculpt clay, and invent their own colorful stories.

🌱

Nature Connection

Our green garden and outdoor play areas allow toddlers to touch wet soil, water saplings, and develop reverence for living things.

🤝

Social Emotional EQ

Through shared toys, circle time, and group celebrations like Bihu and Fruit Day, kids learn sharing, empathy, and kindness.
